Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This tool is private. Its only user is its owner, who signs in with his own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool reads and organizes the owner's own Google mail, contacts, calendar and files, and prepares drafts for the owner to review.

Where the data goes

The data stays on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google accounts. It is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising. Parts of it may be processed by an AI assistant service that the owner uses, only to carry out the owner's own requests.

Storage and removal

Sign-in permissions are stored on the owner's computer. The owner can remove the tool's access at any time in his Google Account settings, under third-party access.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions go to the owner of this page through its GitHub account.
